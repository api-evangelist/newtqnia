Karl Deisseroth, Peter Hegemann and Georg Nagel won the 2026 medicine Nobel for discoveries that created optogenetics, a method for controlling selected neurons with light. The technique transformed causal brain research, but most therapeutic uses still require experimental gene delivery and light hardware.
