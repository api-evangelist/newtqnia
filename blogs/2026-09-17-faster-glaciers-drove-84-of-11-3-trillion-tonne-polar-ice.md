A record combining 42 estimates from 27 satellite missions finds that Greenland and Antarctica lost 11.309 trillion tonnes of ice from 1979 to 2023. Faster glacier discharge caused 84% of the loss, but the historical reconstruction does not determine how quickly future sea level will rise.
