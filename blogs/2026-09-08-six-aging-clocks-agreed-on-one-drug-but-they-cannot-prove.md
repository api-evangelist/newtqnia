Six protein-based aging clocks judged blood samples from 42 lung-disease patients as biologically younger after rentosertib treatment. The agreement is intriguing, but this secondary analysis cannot show that the AI-designed drug slowed aging rather than changing proteins affected by pulmonary fibrosis.
