A gallium-based cathode changed how lithium activates nitrogen in laboratory ammonia synthesis. The team reported 96 ± 6% faradaic efficiency and estimated at least 22% energy efficiency, but has not yet demonstrated a scaled, durable electrolyser or competitive production cost.
