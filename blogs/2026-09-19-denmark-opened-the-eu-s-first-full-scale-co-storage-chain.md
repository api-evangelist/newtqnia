Greensand has opened the EU’s first full-scale carbon-storage chain, moving liquefied CO₂ from Danish biomethane plants to a depleted North Sea oilfield. Its initial capacity is up to 400,000 tonnes a year, but that is capacity, not verified annual storage already achieved.
