Google plans to invest at least €13 billion in Finnish AI infrastructure and signed a 22-year agreement covering up to half of Fortum’s Loviisa nuclear capacity. The contract supports plant upgrades, but the data centres are unbuilt and their total electricity and water requirements remain undisclosed.
