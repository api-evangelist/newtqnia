Henri Kagan and Kenso Soai won the 2026 Chemistry Nobel for showing how reactions can favour and amplify one molecular mirror image. Their work reshaped asymmetric synthesis and drug-making, but the prize honours decades-old foundational discoveries rather than a newly released medicine.
