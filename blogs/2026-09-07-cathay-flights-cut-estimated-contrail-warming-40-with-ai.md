More than 80 Cathay Pacific flights following AI-generated altitude guidance produced an estimated 40% reduction in contrail warming impact, according to Google’s satellite analysis. A larger Asia-Pacific trial is beginning, but the companies have not disclosed a controlled comparison, fuel penalty or independent validation for the initial result.
