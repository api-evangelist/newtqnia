In 1,199 Danish adults, gut-microbiota composition explained more variation than age across most inflammatory and physiological measures. One low-diversity pattern resembled an inflammation profile seen up to 37 years later and predicted more diagnoses, but the observational study cannot establish cause or support a consumer aging test.
