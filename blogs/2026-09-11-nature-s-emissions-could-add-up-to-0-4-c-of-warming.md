A new analysis estimates that emissions from thawing permafrost, wildfires, wetlands and freshwaters could add 0.2°C to 0.4°C of warming by 2100. The feedback is missing from most policy models, but its wide uncertainty and dependence on human emissions mean the higher figure is not inevitable.
