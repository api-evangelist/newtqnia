A Cell study identified adjacent SEMA4A-positive tumour cells, AQP9-positive macrophages and LOXL2-positive fibroblasts that formed a cachexia-promoting niche before measurable tissue loss in pancreatic-cancer models. The finding suggests future markers and targets, but it has not produced a patient test or treatment.
